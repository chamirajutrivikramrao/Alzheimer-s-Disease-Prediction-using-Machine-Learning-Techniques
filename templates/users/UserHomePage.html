About Alzheimer’s Disease

Understanding the disease helps in early detection and better management

What is Alzheimer’s Disease?

Alzheimer’s disease is a progressive neurological disorder that causes gradual loss of memory, thinking ability, and cognitive skills. It is the most common cause of dementia among older adults and affects daily functioning over time.

Causes & Risk Factors
  • Increasing age (major risk factor)
  • Family history and genetic predisposition
  • Low educational attainment
  • Cardiovascular conditions (cholesterol, hypertension)
  • Head injuries and depression
  • Lifestyle factors such as physical inactivity
Common Symptoms
  • Memory loss affecting daily activities
  • Confusion and disorientation
  • Difficulty completing familiar tasks
  • Personality and mood changes
  • Reduced problem-solving ability
Importance of Early Detection

Early identification of Alzheimer’s disease allows timely intervention, better symptom management, and improved quality of life. Machine learning models can assist clinicians by analyzing multiple health indicators to estimate disease risk at an early stage.
